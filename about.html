About aivoicecalc

aivoicecalc is a free tool that helps people estimate what an AI voice agent will cost before they build one. It compares Vapi, Retell AI, Bland AI and Synthflow fee by fee.

How we keep it useful

We base estimates on public pricing, show the date we last reviewed it, and link to each provider's own pricing page. Some links may be affiliate links. They never change the numbers shown.

Corrections

Spotted an outdated price? Tell us and we will check it.
