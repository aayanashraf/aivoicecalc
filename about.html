About AI Voice Calc

AI Voice Calc was built to provide developers, startup founders, and enterprise engineering teams with transparent, real-time cost estimations for building next-generation Voice AI agents.

Our Calculation Methodology

We continuously model real-time per-minute operational rates across the entire Voice AI pipeline:

  • Speech-to-Text (STT): Industry rates from Deepgram, OpenAI Whisper, and AssemblyAI.
  • LLM Intelligence: Token-to-minute operational conversions for GPT-4o mini, Claude 3.5 Haiku, and Realtime APIs.
  • Text-to-Speech (TTS): High-fidelity audio synthesis pricing from ElevenLabs, Cartesia, and PlayHT.
  • Telephony & Orchestration: SIP trunking and orchestration rates via Vapi, Retell AI, and Twilio Direct.
