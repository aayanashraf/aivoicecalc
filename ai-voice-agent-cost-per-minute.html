AI voice agent cost per minute: what you really pay

Last reviewed: October 2026. Figures are estimates. Check provider pricing pages.

The price on a platform's homepage is rarely the price you pay. A voice agent call is built from five parts, and each one adds to the per-minute cost.

The five cost parts

  • Platform fee: what the voice platform charges to run the call.
  • Speech-to-text: turns the caller's speech into text.
  • Language model: decides what the agent says. Bigger models cost more.
  • Voice engine: turns text into speech. Premium voices cost the most.
  • Telephony: the phone line and minutes, often from Twilio or a similar carrier.

The simple formula

Cost per minute = platform + speech-to-text + LLM + voice + telephony. Monthly cost = cost per minute × monthly minutes. Cost per call = monthly cost ÷ number of calls.

Bundled vs unbundled pricing

Some platforms bill each part separately, so you can pick cheaper pieces. Others charge one all-in rate that includes most parts. Bundled pricing is easier to predict. Unbundled pricing gives you more control.

How to lower the cost

  • Pick a smaller model for simple tasks.
  • Compare voice engines on your own script.
  • Keep prompts short and end calls once the goal is met.
  • Ask for committed-volume pricing at steady volume.
