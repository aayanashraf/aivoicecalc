Frequently Asked Questions

How is Infrastructure COGS calculated?

COGS aggregates per-minute pricing from your chosen voice platform (Vapi/Retell), LLM (OpenAI/Groq), Text-to-Speech (ElevenLabs/Cartesia), STT, and telecom carrier trunking fees.

What is a healthy agency profit markup?

Most AI Automation Agencies (AAAs) target a 35% to 50% profit margin on top of raw infrastructure costs, alongside a one-time onboarding setup fee.

Why does voice latency matter?

Conversational latency under 600ms feels completely human-like. Higher latencies create awkward pauses during phone calls, affecting client conversion rates.

Can I use this for multi-currency invoicing?

Yes! Use the currency dropdown at the top right to instantly convert all estimates into USD, EUR, GBP, SAR, AED, or INR.
