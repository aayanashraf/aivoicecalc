Who this calculator is for

Agencies pricing voice agents for clients, founders choosing a platform, and teams moving from a pilot to real call volume. Appointment booking usually means short calls, support lines mean medium calls, and outbound sales means many calls with a few minutes each. Enter your own call length to see how each shape changes the bill.

Five ways to cut voice agent cost

  • Use a smaller language model for simple tasks such as booking.
  • Test cheaper voice engines on your own script before you commit.
  • Keep prompts short. Long prompts raise LLM cost on every turn.
  • End calls quickly when the goal is met.
  • Ask providers for committed-volume pricing once you pass a steady monthly volume.

How the estimate works

Most platforms advertise one platform fee. A real call also pays for speech-to-text, a language model, a voice engine and phone minutes. Vapi and Retell bill these separately, so the calculator adds them up. Bland and Synthflow bundle more of the stack, so the calculator treats their rates differently. Per-call cost is your monthly total divided by calls. For a closer look, read Vapi vs Retell.

Frequently asked questions

Vapi vs Retell cost: which is cheaper?

Both charge a per-minute platform fee and pass through LLM, voice, speech-to-text and telephony. Vapi's platform fee is usually lower, but your model and voice choices change the total more than the platform does. Adjust the inputs above to compare at your volume.

What are ElevenLabs per minute rates for voice agents?

ElevenLabs is typically one of the pricier voice engines. Deepgram and Cartesia are often much cheaper, with different voice quality. Test the voices before you decide.

How much does Twilio add per minute?

US outbound calls on Twilio run about $0.014 per minute plus number rental. Some platforms bundle telephony into their rate.

Are these prices exact?

No. They are planning estimates. Providers change pricing often, so confirm current rates on each provider's pricing page.
