Vapi vs Retell AI: which costs less?

Last reviewed: October 2026. Rates change often, so confirm on each pricing page.

Vapi and Retell AI both build voice agents from separate parts: speech-to-text, a language model, a voice engine and phone minutes. Both charge a platform fee on top. Your real cost depends mostly on the model and voice you choose.

How each one charges

VapiRetell AI
Platform feeStarts at $0.05 per minutePublished starting rates are around $0.07 per minute, depending on voice and model
Speech, LLM, voiceBilled at the provider's price, no markupVaries by the voice and model you pick
Phone minutesBilled separatelyBilled separately or via your own carrier
Best forDevelopers who want control over each partTeams who want a faster setup

What to compare before you choose

  • Voice quality and latency on your own script.
  • Which parts are billed separately and which are bundled.
  • Concurrency limits, monthly base plans and add-ons.
  • Volume discounts at higher usage.
